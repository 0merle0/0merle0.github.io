Hi, I'm Jacob.

Building tools for protein design.
the future is sustainable.

Currently, I'm a machine learning scientist at Prescient Design (Genentech, Roche), where I work on post-training structure prediction models and agentic workflows for drug discovery.

I'm interested in developing new technology for protein design, especially active learning that cycles between models and experimental data, and in using protein design to tackle grand challenges that move us toward a more sustainable society.

I recently finished my PhD in the Baker lab at the University of Washington Institute for Protein Design, where I worked on sequence–structure co-generation and enzyme library design. A big focus of my work was finding ways to annotate protein sequence–function space more efficiently by designing scalable, cost-effective libraries. Before that, I did my undergraduate and master’s research in the Hookway lab at SUNY Binghamton, engineering 3D cardiac microtissues.

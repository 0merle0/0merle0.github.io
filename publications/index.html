  2. bioRxiv
    Computational design of cysteine proteases
    Hojae Choi, Brian Coventry, Magnus Bauer, and 15 more authors
    bioRxiv, 2025
    Abs DOI

    De novo design of enzymes that use an activated cysteine nucleophile to hydrolyze the polypeptide backbone in a sequence-dependent manner, achieving rate enhancements over the background reaction of up to 3 x 10^7. The designs adopt folds very different from natural proteases, and crystal structures closely match the design models.

  3. arXiv
    Hierarchical protein backbone generation with latent and structure diffusion
    Jason Yim, Marouane Jaakik, Ge Liu, and 5 more authors
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2504.09374, 2025
    Abs

    LSD is a two-stage generative model for protein backbones: latents are sampled and decoded into a contact map, then atomic coordinates are sampled conditioned on that contact map. The hierarchy enables new forms of control over generation while scaling to large datasets.

  4. Nat. Biotech.
    Multistate and functional protein design using RoseTTAFold sequence space diffusion
    Sidney Lyayuga Lisanza, Jacob Merle Gershon, Samuel W. K. Tipps, and 16 more authors
    Nature Biotechnology, 2025
    Abs DOI HTML Code

    ProteinGenerator is a sequence space diffusion model built on RoseTTAFold that generates protein sequences and structures simultaneously by iterative denoising, guided by desired sequence and structural attributes. We design thermostable proteins with varying amino acid compositions and internal sequence repeats, and cage bioactive peptides such as melittin.
